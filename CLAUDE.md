# Context for Claude

- **Brand: Cult.** This repo and the "Cult Brief to Board" dashboard (https://claude.ai/artifact/BngoWsHz2gLs79eYZUewZj) serve the brand **Cult**. Products are sold through Cultstore, but every creative, reference and art direction is for Cult.
- Follow Cult's visual guidelines (stored in the dashboard at `brand/guide`): Max Energy #F2FF00, Power Pop #FF5353, Deep Charge #0095FF, black, white; Sora headlines + Caveat accent + Inter utility; rounded-corner frame; joy over grit; product in use; fitness for every body; subtle Indian everyday culture.
- When picking references for a brief, judge them against Cult's guideline, read the viewer's taste profile (`data/users/me/taste`) and liked/passed ratings first, and aim for at least 50 references per brief.
- See `brief-to-board/README.md` for the dashboard's data model.
